About Our Looms & Heritage

Rooted in Tradition, Built for Modern Living

At Jagdish Handloom Carpets, every piece carries the legacy of generational artisans from Panipat, Haryana — woven with authentic pit & frame looms, uncompromised natural fibres, and meticulous hand-knotting.

Founder & Master Artisan

Mr. Jagdish Chaurasiya

Owner & Master Craftsman

With over 30 years of generational mastery at authentic handlooms, Mr. Jagdish Chaurasiya established the manufacturing foundation in Panipat, Haryana. His exacting standards in raw yarn curation, knot density, and warp-weft alignment preserve timeless Indian handloom artistry.

Spearheading modern operations and nationwide distribution, Vindhayachal Chaurasiya oversees bespoke wholesale orders, custom interior projects, export-grade quality assurance, and seamless client partnerships across India.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to common questions regarding wholesale supply, bespoke weaving, minimum orders, and dispatch schedules.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for wholesale orders? +

For standard handloom carpets and flatweave dhurries, our wholesale MOQ starts at 15 to 20 pieces (or 100 sq. meters). For bespoke or custom-designed hotel/hospitality projects, we accept initial trial orders starting from 10 pieces.

Can we request custom sizes, specific patterns, and color motifs? +

Yes, absolutely. Over 40% of our daily loom production is fully custom-made. You can share your CAD drawings, Pantone shade numbers, or sample swatches. We can weave oversized rugs (up to 14×20 ft), hallway runners, round carpets, and wall-to-wall pieces strictly to your specifications.

Do you provide sample swatches before bulk manufacturing? +

Yes. For bulk wholesale buyers, dealers, and export orders, we craft and courier a sample quality swatch (typically 1×1 ft or 2×2 ft) for density, weave texture, and dye approval before initiating full commercial production.

What materials, wool blends, and yarns do you use? +

We work with 100% pure Indian & New Zealand blended wool, pure cotton dhurrie yarns, high-density chenille, jute-cotton blends, and viscose silk accents. All our yarns undergo professional colourfast testing to prevent bleeding and fading.

What is your production and dispatch turnaround time? +

Ready-stock inventory dispatches within 24 to 48 hours from our Panipat warehouse. For custom bulk loom orders, production typically takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on quantity, knot density, and finishing specifications.

Do you supply across India and support international exports? +

Yes! We supply wholesalers and project developers across all Indian states with door-to-door freight partners. For overseas buyers, we provide export-standard moisture barrier packaging, fumigation certificates, and dispatch via major Indian ports (Mundra, Nhava Sheva, or Delhi ICD).
